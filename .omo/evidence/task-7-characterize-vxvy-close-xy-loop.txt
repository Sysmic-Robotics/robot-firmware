4. WHY THE WORST-CASE PLANT CANNOT MEET THE GATES (documented finding)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the identified plant (K=0.381, L+T=340 ms, clamp +-2.0 m/s) the 0.5 m
step is ACTUATOR/PLANT-LIMITED, not controller-limited:
  * max actual velocity = K * output_max = 0.381*2.0 = 0.76 m/s
    -> the 63% midpoint (0.315 m) needs >= 0.41 s at full speed plus the
       340 ms inner lag to build it -> ~0.7 s rise floor;
  * stopping travel ~= v_max*(L+T) = 0.76*0.34 ~= 0.26 m before braking
    develops -> ~20% overshoot floor.
Confirmed by sweeps: lambda 0.8->1.7 gives rise 733-900ms / OS 19.6-23.9%
(never both under target), and moving the PI zero (Ki/Kp) from 0.5/lambda
to 1.5/lambda monotonically raises OS (21.5% -> 45%).  No Kp/Ki choice in
this Kp/Ki/Kff structure beats these floors.
The SAME gains PASS both gates on the plausible true plant (K is a LOWER
bound, L/T are UPPER bounds, so the real robot is faster/stronger — center
of the plausible range: rise 483 ms, OS 13.8%).  This is reported honestly
in the tool output + JSON rather than silently tightening lambda below the
3-5x design band (the earlier auto-tighten drove lambda to 0.63 s and STILL
failed, while violating the cascade separation rule).
